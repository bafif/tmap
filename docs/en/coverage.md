### Limits of the population data

- Per-country series use present-day borders: a historical region smaller or larger than its countries (the Rus, Mesoamerica, the Andes) gets the population of the whole countries, and each series' note says so.
- There can be a jump between a curated estimate and the per-country series: they're different methods and territories, and the chart shows the source of each point.
- Our World in Data doesn't publish HYDE's low and high scenarios, so the series have no uncertainty range. The HYDE grids, which would make it possible to add up the population inside each polygon, are on Utrecht University's server behind an anti-bot check.
